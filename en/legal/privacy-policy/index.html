General Information on Data Processing

Hosting and Provision of the Website
#

This website is provided as a static website via GitHub Pages. When the website is accessed, technically necessary access data is processed, in particular:

  • IP address
  • date and time of access
  • pages or files accessed
  • referrer URL
  • browser and device information
  • status codes

This processing is carried out for the purpose of technically providing the website, ensuring stability, and preventing misuse or attacks.

We do not operate our own web servers and do not maintain our own server log files. To the extent that technical access data is logged, this takes place within the scope of hosting by GitHub.

In connection with hosting, personal data may also be processed in third countries, in particular in the United States. GitHub states that data may also be processed in the United States and refers in this context in particular to Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework as possible transfer mechanisms.

The domain of this website is managed via ALL-INKL.COM. In the course of domain management, technical DNS provision, and, where applicable, e-mail configuration, data may be processed where necessary for operation, administration, and security.

Contacting Us (by E-mail)

If you contact us by e-mail, we process the data you provide, in particular:

Cookies and Similar Technologies
#

We currently do not use cookies for analytics, marketing, or advertising purposes.

However, this website uses your browser’s LocalStorage for certain functions. In doing so, simple status values may be stored and read, for example whether a particular article has already been viewed in this browser or marked with “Like”. This information is stored locally in your browser and can be deleted if you remove your browser cache or stored website data. Depending on your browser settings or when using private mode, this data may also be deleted automatically when the browser is closed.

Use of Analytics Tools

Umami

We use Umami for privacy-friendly statistical analysis of the use of our website and for audience measurement.

According to Umami, the service works without cookies, without fingerprinting, and without collecting personal data. Social Share Functions
#

Where sharing functions for social networks or messengers are offered on this website, they are currently provided as simple links and not as embedded social media plugins. Simply visiting our website does not automatically establish a connection to the respective social network.

Only when you actively click such a link do you leave our website, and the privacy policies of the respective provider then apply.

Use of Firebase / Cloud Firestore
#

For certain functions of this website, we use Firebase / Cloud Firestore by Google. We use Cloud Firestore only to process technical information required for displaying view counts and “Like” information for individual articles.

We do not use Firebase for analytics, advertising, or marketing purposes and do not use any other Firebase services such as Analytics.

Simple status values are stored in the user’s browser via LocalStorage. These are used to keep track, within the browser being used, of whether a particular article has already been viewed or whether a “Like” has already been set for an article. This is intended in particular to prevent the same action from being counted unnecessarily multiple times within the same browser.

This information is stored locally in the browser and, according to our design, does not contain any real names or direct contact details. It can be deleted if the browser cache or stored website data is removed. Depending on the browser settings or when using private mode, this data may also be deleted automatically when the browser is closed.

In connection with the use of Firebase / Cloud Firestore, personal data may also be processed in third countries, in particular in the United States. Firebase is subject to Google’s privacy and contractual terms. Google provides, among other things, Data Processing and Security Terms as well as Standard Contractual Clauses for Firebase for this purpose.
